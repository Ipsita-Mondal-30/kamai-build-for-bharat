How it works

Speak

"आज 1800 की बिक्री और 1100 का माल." Hindi, Hinglish or English. Each line is understood and sealed into her ledger.

Prove

Every entry is chained to the last. UPI and receipts cross-check it. A lender can verify; Savitri can't fake it.

Protect

When real rain empties her stall, her own ledger plus the rainfall record files the insurance claim. No bills. No forms.

Honest about what's real

Real
  • Daily rainfall at the stall's coordinates (Open-Meteo / ERA5, Jul–Sep 2026).
  • Hindi/Hinglish voice parsing, tamper-evident hash chain, scoring, fraud checks, loan sizing and claim logic, all in engine.js and tested.
  • Loan modelled on MUDRA-Shishu (up to ₹50,000), EMI ≤ 35% of a stressed income (weakest 20% of weeks).
Simulated, and how production differs
  • Savitri's sales, UPI and receipts are generated. Production: UPI inflows via Account Aggregator, receipts via OCR of supplier slips.
  • Fingerprints use a fast demo hash. Production: SHA-256 with periodic anchoring so even Kamai can't rewrite history.
  • Policy terms are illustrative. Score is a transparent weighted model until repayment data exists to train one.
