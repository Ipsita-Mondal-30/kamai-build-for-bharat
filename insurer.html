Why no assessor?
  • Rainfall is read from a public weather record at the vendor's coordinates, not self-reported.
  • Sales loss comes from a hash-chained ledger. If the chain is broken the claim is held, not paid.
  • Payout = 70% of the sales shortfall, max ₹800/day, 4 days/month.
  • Honest note: this is the monsoon quarter, the worst three months. The break-even premium above is far more than a vendor can pay, so a pilot would fund the cover through the loan (the lender benefits from lower defaults) or a subsidy, and average it over dry months too.
